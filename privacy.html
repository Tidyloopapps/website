Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains how Tidyloop Apps ("we") handles information when a merchant installs one of our Shopify apps, including Tidyloop Split.

Information we access

When you install an app, Shopify grants it access to the store data needed to work. For Tidyloop Split this is: order and fulfillment order details (order ID, order number, line items, quantities, product weights, tags, vendor, product type, SKU and locations), and basic store information (store name, domain, plan, contact email).

Information we store

  • Your app settings and rules
  • A log of split and hold actions: order ID, order number, fulfillment order IDs, date and result
  • Monthly usage counts for billing
  • The access credentials Shopify issues to the app

We do not store customer names, addresses, email addresses, phone numbers or payment details.

How we use it

Only to provide the app: applying your rules to your orders, showing your activity log, enforcing plan limits and answering support requests. We do not sell or rent data, use it for advertising, or share it except with service providers that host the app (cloud hosting and database), under confidentiality obligations.

Retention and deletion

When you uninstall, your data is deleted within 48 hours. We respond to Shopify's customer data request and redaction requests as required.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access restricted to Tidyloop Apps.

Your rights

You can request access to or deletion of your data at any time by emailing support@tidyloopapps.com. If you are in the EEA, UK or California you may have additional rights under local law; contact us to exercise them.

Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date above.
