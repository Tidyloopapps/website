Tidyloop Split — help

Set up in 3 steps

  1. Create a rule. In Shopify admin open Apps → Tidyloop Split → Add rule. Pick what to match (tag, vendor, product type, SKU, out of stock, preorder tag, or order weight) and what to do (ship separately, or hold until a date).
  2. Turn it on. Rules run from top to bottom on every new order. Each item uses the first rule it matches. Items that match no rule stay together.
  3. Check an order. Open the next matching order. You'll see separate fulfillment groups on the order page, and an entry in Tidyloop Split → Activity.

Common setups

  • Preorders: tag preorder products "preorder" → rule "Product tag is preorder" → Hold until your release date. The in-stock items ship now.
  • Dropship vendor: rule "Vendor is [vendor name]" → Ship separately. Send that fulfillment group to the vendor.
  • Personalized items: tag them (for example "engraved") → Ship separately, so made-to-order items don't hold up the rest.
  • Heavy orders: rule "Order weight over 20 lb" → Split into packages under 20 lb. Uses the product weights in Shopify.

Releasing holds

Holds with a date release automatically on that date. To release early, open Activity and click Release, or remove the hold on the order page in Shopify.

Split an existing order

On any order page, open More actions → Split now. Your current rules are applied to that order.

What it never does

It never creates new orders, refunds, or changes what the customer paid. Splits use Shopify's fulfillment orders inside the original order.

Plan limits

When you reach your plan's monthly limit, new orders are left as they are (never blocked) and the app shows a notice. Splitting resumes next month or after upgrading.

Uninstalling

Splits already made stay in place. Before uninstalling, release any active holds in Activity so those items can ship. Your rules and log are deleted within 48 hours of uninstalling.
