How to ship oversized and fragile items separately in Shopify

Updated November 26, 2026

Some products can't go in the same box as everything else: a bike frame that ships by freight, a mirror that needs its own crate, a rug that goes as an oversized parcel. When one order mixes these with small items, the warehouse has to pack them apart anyway. The goal is two shipments inside one order, each with its own tracking, with no second order and no refund.

What Shopify does on its own

  • Shopify puts every item from the same location into one fulfillment group, so the oversized item and the small items show up as one shipment to pack.
  • You can split them by hand: on the order page, use Fulfill items for just the small items, then fulfill the large item separately with its own carrier and tracking number.
  • Shopify Flow can react to new orders, but it has no action that moves certain items into their own fulfillment group.

Splitting by hand works when large items are rare. When they are a regular part of your catalog, packers have to read every order to catch them.

Doing it automatically

  1. Give oversized products a shared product type, for example Oversized or Freight. If you already use product types for something else, use a product tag instead.
  2. Add a rule: "When an item has the product type Oversized, ship it separately."
  3. For fragile items, add a second rule with its own product type or tag, for example Fragile.

Tidyloop Split applies those rules to each new order inside the same order. The small items stay in the normal shipment; each oversized or fragile item gets its own fulfillment group, so packers see it as a separate shipment and the customer gets separate tracking. "Ship separately" works on every plan.

If the problem is total weight rather than size (many small heavy items), use a weight limit instead: see How to split heavy Shopify orders into multiple packages by weight.

Good practice

  • Set the rule on the product (type or tag), not on the order, so every future order is covered.
  • Set real package weights and dimensions on large products, so carrier rates at checkout match what you ship.
  • Say on the product page that large items ship separately, so a second tracking email isn't a surprise.
  • For several product types (Furniture, Freight, Oversized), list them in one rule, separated by commas.
