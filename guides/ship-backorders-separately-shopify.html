How to ship in-stock items first and backorders later in Shopify

Updated November 25, 2026

If you keep selling products after they run out, some orders will mix items you have on the shelf with items you don't. Waiting for everything holds up the whole order. Shipping what you have now and sending the rest later keeps customers happy, but Shopify doesn't separate those items for you.

Step 1: Let products sell when they're out of stock

  1. In Shopify admin open Products and choose the product (or the variant).
  2. In the Inventory section make sure Track quantity is on.
  3. Turn on Continue selling when out of stock and save.

The available quantity can now go below zero. A negative number means that many units are sold but not yet in stock.

Step 2: Split the order after checkout

By hand: open the order, use Fulfill items to ship only the in-stock items, and leave the backordered items unfulfilled until stock arrives. That works for a few orders a week, but you have to check stock on every order, and nothing on the order shows which part is waiting.

Automatically: Tidyloop Split checks each new order. With the rule Item is out of stock → Ship separately, any item that sold with no stock left goes into its own shipment, and the rest of the order stays ready to ship. On the Pro plan you can also put the backordered shipment on hold so nobody packs it by mistake, then release it with one click when the stock comes in. It stays one order, so the customer pays shipping once and your reports don't change.

Tips

  • Say on the product page or in the cart that backordered items ship separately, and give an expected date if you have one.
  • Products with Track quantity off never count as out of stock, because Shopify has no number to check.
  • For items you'll never stock until a launch date, a preorder tag and a hold until that date works better. See How to ship preorders separately.
