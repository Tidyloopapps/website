How to split heavy Shopify orders into multiple packages by weight

Updated October 29, 2026

Carriers and shipping services often cap the weight of one package. When an order goes over that, your team has to divide it by hand before printing labels, or the label fails.

What Shopify does on its own

  • Shopify keeps all items from one location in one fulfillment group, whatever they weigh.
  • You can fulfill some items now and the rest later, but you choose which items go together each time.
  • Shopify Flow has no action that splits a fulfillment group, so it can't do this automatically.

Before you start: set product weights

Weight-based splitting is only as good as your product data. In each product's Shipping section, enter the weight of one unit including its own packaging. Items with no weight are treated as weighing nothing.

Splitting by weight automatically

With Tidyloop Split, add an order-weight rule with a limit such as 20 kg (or 44 lb). Each new order's items are packed into shipments that stay under the limit, inside the same order. An item that is heavier than the limit on its own gets its own shipment. Weight rules are included on the Pro and Unlimited plans.

Good practice

  • Set the limit a little under the carrier's maximum to leave room for the box and filler.
  • Combine with a vendor or tag rule if some heavy items ship from a supplier.
  • Check the first few heavy orders on the order page to confirm the shipments look right.
