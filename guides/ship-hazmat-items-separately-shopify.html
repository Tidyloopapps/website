How to ship hazmat items separately in Shopify (batteries, aerosols, perfume)

Updated November 26, 2026

Lithium batteries, aerosol cans, nail polish, perfume and other items classed as dangerous goods often have to travel by ground and carry special markings. When a customer orders one of these with ordinary items, packing everything in one box forces the whole order onto the slower, restricted service. The goal is two shipments inside one order: the hazmat item by ground with the right label, the rest by your normal service.

What Shopify does on its own

  • Shopify puts every item from the same location into one fulfillment group, so the hazmat item and the ordinary items show up as one shipment to pack.
  • You can split them by hand: on the order page, use Fulfill items for the ordinary items, then fulfill the hazmat item on its own with a ground service and the required markings.
  • Shopify Flow can tag or flag orders that contain hazmat products, but it has no action that moves those items into their own fulfillment group.

Splitting by hand works for a few orders a week. When hazmat products are a regular part of your catalog, every packer has to spot them on every order, and one miss can mean a returned or seized parcel.

Doing it automatically

  1. Tag every hazmat product with a shared product tag, for example Hazmat. You can also use separate tags such as Lithium and Aerosol if they need different handling.
  2. Add a rule: "When an item has the tag Hazmat, ship it separately."

Tidyloop Split applies that rule to each new order inside the same order. Ordinary items stay in the normal shipment; each hazmat item gets its own fulfillment group, so packers see it as a separate shipment and can buy the right ground label for it. "Ship separately" works on every plan.

Good practice

  • Check your carrier's dangerous goods rules for each product. This guide covers splitting the order, not which items count as hazmat or how to label them.
  • Tag the product, not the order, so every future order is covered.
  • Set shipping profiles or rates so hazmat products are only offered ground shipping at checkout.
  • Say on the product page that the item ships separately by ground, so a slower second package isn't a surprise.
