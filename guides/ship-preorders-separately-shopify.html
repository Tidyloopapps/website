How to ship preorders separately in Shopify and charge shipping once

Updated October 29, 2026

When a customer buys a preorder item and an in-stock item in the same order, you usually want to send the in-stock item now and the preorder later. Shopify gives you two ways to do that, and they treat shipping charges differently.

Option 1: Shopify's split shipping in checkout

Shopify can split an order into separate shipments at checkout when items can't ship together, including preorders, items at different locations, and items in different shipping profiles. The customer sees each shipment and picks a shipping method for each one, so they can be charged shipping more than once.

Where your store has the setting, it's in Settings → Shipping and delivery → Show split shipping in checkout. Stores using shipping options by market have it on all the time.

This works if you're happy for customers to pay for each shipment. Many merchants aren't: customers ask for one shipment and a refund of the second shipping fee.

Option 2: One shipping charge, split after checkout

  1. Turn off Show split shipping in checkout (if your store has the toggle), so the customer pays shipping once.
  2. After the order is placed, move the preorder items into their own fulfillment group and hold it until the release date.

Shopify's built-in hold applies to a whole shipment, not to individual items, and you'd set it order by order.

An app can do it automatically. Tidyloop Split checks every new order: items tagged as preorder go into their own shipment on hold until the date you choose, and the rest stay ready to ship. It stays one order, so reports and order editing work as normal.

Tips

  • Tag preorder products consistently (for example preorder) so rules can find them.
  • Say on the product page that preorder items ship separately on the release date.
  • If a preorder date moves, change the hold date in one place instead of editing each order.
