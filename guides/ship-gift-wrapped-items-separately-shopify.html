How to ship gift-wrapped items separately in Shopify

Updated November 26, 2026

Gift wrapping is usually done at a separate table, by one person, at a slower pace than normal packing. When a customer orders a wrapped gift together with everyday items, packing everything in one box means the whole order waits for the wrapping station, and the wrapped gift can get crushed or opened by mistake. The goal is two shipments inside one order: the wrapped items packed on their own, the rest shipped as soon as it is picked.

What Shopify does on its own

  • Shopify puts every item from the same location into one fulfillment group, so wrapped and unwrapped items show up as one shipment to pack.
  • Gift wrap is often sold as a separate product or a line item property; Shopify shows it on the order but does not change how the order is fulfilled.
  • You can split by hand: on the order page, use Fulfill items for the unwrapped items first, then fulfill the wrapped items when the wrapping station is done.

Splitting by hand works in a quiet week. In the holiday rush, every packer has to notice the gift wrap on every order, and a missed one either delays the whole order or ships a gift unwrapped.

Doing it automatically

  1. Give the products you wrap, or your gift-wrap variant, a shared product tag, for example Gift wrap.
  2. Add a rule: "When an item has the tag Gift wrap, ship it separately."

Tidyloop Split applies that rule to each new order inside the same order. Unwrapped items stay in the normal shipment; each wrapped item gets its own fulfillment group, so packers see it as a separate shipment to send to the wrapping station. "Ship separately" works on every plan.

Tidyloop Split does not wrap, buy postage or pick a carrier. It only separates the items so each shipment can be packed in the right place.

Good practice

  • Tag the product or variant, not the order, so every future order is covered.
  • Decide whether you charge one shipping fee or two, and say on the cart or product page that wrapped gifts may arrive in a separate box.
  • Leave packing slips with prices out of the wrapped box if the gift goes straight to the recipient.
  • Before the holiday season, run one test order with a wrapped and an unwrapped item to check the split.
