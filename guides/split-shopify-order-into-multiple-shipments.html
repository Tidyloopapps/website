How to split a Shopify order into multiple shipments

Updated November 25, 2026

One order often can't go out in one box: a preorder sits next to an in-stock item, two vendors ship from different places, or the order is too heavy for one package. Shopify lets you ship an order in parts, but it doesn't decide which parts for you. Here are three ways to do it, from fully manual to automatic.

Option 1: Fulfill part of the order by hand

  1. In Shopify admin open Orders and choose the order.
  2. Click Fulfill items.
  3. Lower the quantity of each item you are not shipping yet (set it to 0), add tracking if you have it, and confirm.

The items you shipped are marked fulfilled. The rest stay on the order as unfulfilled, and you repeat the steps when they are ready. This is fine for a few orders a week. The downside: someone has to look at every order and remember what is waiting.

Option 2: Put the waiting items on hold

If part of the order must not ship yet, a hold stops anyone from packing it by mistake. Shopify can hold a fulfillment from the order page, and the hold stays until someone releases it. See How to hold part of a Shopify order and ship the rest for the steps and the catches.

Option 3: Split orders automatically with rules

Tidyloop Split checks each new order and splits it into separate shipments by rules you set, for example:

  • Product tag is "preorder" → Ship separately (and on Pro, hold until the release date).
  • Item is out of stock → Ship separately, so the in-stock part goes out now.
  • Vendor is "Acme" → Ship separately, for dropshipping or a second warehouse partner.
  • Max 20 lb per package (Pro), so heavy orders are cut into boxes that fit your carrier.

It stays one order: the customer pays once, your reports don't change, and each shipment gets its own tracking. Orders placed before you set up a rule can be split from the order page with More actions → Split now.

Which one to use

  • A handful of mixed orders a month: Option 1.
  • Some items regularly wait for a date or for stock: Option 2, or Option 3 if you don't want to check each order.
  • The same kind of split every day (preorders, vendors, weight): Option 3.

More detail for each case: preorders, backorders, vendors, personalized items, oversized and fragile items, hazmat items, perishable items, high-value items, alcohol and age-restricted items, samples and swatches, weight.
