How to ship alcohol and age-restricted items separately in Shopify (adult signature)

Updated November 26, 2026

Wine, beer, spirits, vape products and some other items can only be delivered to an adult who shows ID, and carriers charge extra for an adult signature. When a customer orders a bottle of wine with a corkscrew and a gift bag, packing everything together means the whole parcel waits for an adult signature, and a missed delivery sends all of it back. The goal is two shipments inside one order: the age-restricted item with adult signature, the rest by your normal service.

What Shopify does on its own

  • Shopify puts every item from the same location into one fulfillment group, so the age-restricted item and the ordinary items show up as one shipment to pack.
  • You can split them by hand: on the order page, use Fulfill items for the ordinary items, then fulfill the age-restricted item on its own and add adult signature to that label.
  • Shopify Flow can tag or flag orders that contain age-restricted products, but it has no action that moves those items into their own fulfillment group.

Splitting by hand works for a few orders a week. When age-restricted items are a regular part of your catalog, every packer has to spot them on every order, and one miss can mean a delivery that breaks your carrier's terms or the law where the customer lives.

Doing it automatically

  1. Tag every age-restricted product with a shared product tag, for example Adult signature.
  2. Add a rule: "When an item has the tag Adult signature, ship it separately."

Tidyloop Split applies that rule to each new order inside the same order. Ordinary items stay in the normal shipment; each age-restricted item gets its own fulfillment group, so packers see it as a separate shipment that needs an adult signature. "Ship separately" works on every plan.

Tidyloop Split does not check a customer's age or decide where you may ship. It only separates the items so the right shipment gets the right label. Age checks at checkout and shipping-zone limits are separate settings.

Good practice

  • Tag the product, not the order, so every future order is covered.
  • Check your carrier's rules for alcohol and vape products before you ship; many carriers need a signed agreement and limit which states or countries you can ship to.
  • Limit shipping zones in Shopify for age-restricted products to the places you are licensed to ship to.
  • Say on the product page that the item ships separately and needs an adult (21+ in the US) to sign, so the customer can plan to be home.
