How to ship high-value items separately in Shopify (signature and insurance)

Updated November 26, 2026

Jewelry, watches, electronics, collectibles and other expensive items often need extra parcel insurance, a signature on delivery and plain, discreet packaging. When a customer orders one with a few low-cost items, packing everything together means paying insurance on the whole parcel and asking for a signature on a box of socks. The goal is two shipments inside one order: the high-value item insured and signed for, the rest by your normal service.

What Shopify does on its own

  • Shopify puts every item from the same location into one fulfillment group, so the high-value item and the ordinary items show up as one shipment to pack.
  • You can split them by hand: on the order page, use Fulfill items for the ordinary items, then fulfill the high-value item on its own and add insurance and signature confirmation to that label.
  • Shopify Flow can tag or flag orders that contain high-value products, but it has no action that moves those items into their own fulfillment group.

Splitting by hand works for a few orders a week. When expensive items are a regular part of your catalog, every packer has to spot them on every order, and one miss can mean an uninsured loss or a parcel left on a doorstep.

Doing it automatically

  1. Tag every high-value product with a shared product tag, for example High value.
  2. Add a rule: "When an item has the tag High value, ship it separately."

Tidyloop Split applies that rule to each new order inside the same order. Ordinary items stay in the normal shipment; each high-value item gets its own fulfillment group, so packers see it as a separate shipment that needs insurance and a signature. "Ship separately" works on every plan.

If you want to hold expensive items for a manual fraud review before they ship, a Pro plan rule can hold that shipment for a set number of days, while the rest of the order ships now. See How to hold part of a Shopify order and ship the rest.

Good practice

  • Tag the product, not the order, so every future order is covered.
  • Check what your carrier covers by default and when it requires declared value or a signature; buy extra coverage only on the high-value shipment.
  • Use plain outer packaging with no brand or product names on high-value parcels.
  • Say on the product page that the item ships separately and needs a signature, so the customer can plan to be home.
