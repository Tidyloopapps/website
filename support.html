Tidyloop Split

How does splitting work?

The app divides an order's shipments using Shopify's built-in fulfillment orders. The order stays a single order. Nothing is refunded, duplicated or re-charged.

Does it change what the customer paid?

No. Prices, taxes, discounts and payment are untouched.

What happens if I uninstall?

Splits already made stay in place. Before uninstalling, release any active holds in Activity so those items can ship. Your rules and log are deleted within 48 hours of uninstalling.
