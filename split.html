Tidyloop Split

Split and hold shipments inside the same Shopify order, by rules you set.

Some orders contain items that ship differently: a preorder next to an in-stock item, a dropship product, a personalized piece, a heavy item, or stock held at another location. Tidyloop Split applies your rules to each new order and separates those items into their own shipments.

It uses Shopify's own fulfillment orders, so the order stays one order. Customers see the correct status, sales reports stay accurate, and the order can still be edited.

What it does

  • Splits after checkout, so customers pay shipping once
  • Split by product tag, vendor, product type, SKU or stock status
  • Hold preorder or made-to-order items until a date or for a set number of days after each order, then release them automatically or with one click
  • Works with pre-order apps: items bought on a pre-order selling plan are handled automatically
  • Split heavy orders into packages under a weight limit you set
  • Split any order manually from the order page with "Split now"
  • Activity log of every split and hold
  • Works with Shopify Flow: each split fires Flow's "Fulfillment order split" trigger
